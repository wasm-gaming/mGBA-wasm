## The common part (all the engines with a settings screen)

- **engine-specs `^0.5.0` → `^0.6.0`**. The app imports the helpers from `../engine-specs.js`, the
  runtime copy the Makefile already ships beside the SDK.
- **English and Spanish**:
  - every string of the app (today Spanish, inline) moves into one `{ en, es }` table;
  - the table is read through `host.locale ?? resolveLocale()`;
  - SDK errors stay English and are shown as a detail under the app's own words.
- **Pause menu**: Continuar, the engine's entries, **Ajustes**, **Pantalla completa**, Volver al
  lanzador (UX.md rule 4).
- **Ajustes**: `createSettingsScreen()`.
  - Rows come from `settingsModel()` over the SDK's `config()` / `options()` descriptors (only those
    with a `label`), plus `SHARED_SETTINGS`.
  - A live change is applied at once.
  - A non-live one is saved and passed at the next start; its row says "Se aplica al reiniciar".
  - Choices are saved per engine with `saveSettings('mgba')` and read at mount with
    `loadSettings()`.
- **Fullscreen**: `createFullscreenToggle(root)` (Cmd+Enter / Alt+Enter, Select + Y), plus the
  pause-menu entry.
- **Performance**:
  - `createWakeLock()`, held while playing when "Mantener la pantalla encendida" is on (default
    on);
  - `createPerfCounter()` when "Mostrar rendimiento" is on: fed once per emulated frame where the
    SDK reports frames, else counting display frames.
- **Controls and audio pacing**: per engine, below.
- **Checks**:
  - `make test` / build;
  - in Chromium, with the owner's test ROMs where needed: the pause menu, every setting applied (or
    noted as on restart), saved across a reload, fullscreen by key and by menu, the counter, en / es;
  - a phone width.
- **Release**: minor.

## mGBA

- **Settings**: the 11 catalog rows in `src/mgba.options.ts` (Video / Audio / Emulation: image
  filtering, aspect, interframe blending, volume, system, GB model, BIOS…).
  - They already carry English labels and value labels, and become `label` / `group` /
    `valueLabels` with Spanish added.
  - Rows that need a reset stay non-live.
- **Controls**: feasible.
  - `src/mgba.core.ts` maps `KeyboardEvent.code` → control through `DEFAULT_KEYMAP` /
    `buildKeymap()`.
  - The app lists A, B, L, R, Start, Select and the d-pad, and passes the saved bindings to the
    core.
  - Gamepad buttons: to check how the core reads `getGamepads()`. If it takes a button table, the
    pad is remapped too; if not, only keys, and the row says so.
- **Audio pacing** ("Sincronización"): the core drives its own `requestAnimationFrame` loop
  (`mgba.core.ts`). To check whether that loop can take `createFrameClock()`. If it cannot without
  changing the WASM build, the setting is left out of this release and its record says why.
- **Order**: first of the engines, as the template for the rest (it has the newest package
  layout).
